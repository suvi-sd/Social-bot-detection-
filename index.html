How it works

The model turns raw profile numbers into eight behaviour signals. Each signal is scaled to 0–1, multiplied by a fixed weight and summed to a score out of 100. A logistic (sigmoid) function then converts the score to a probability.

    Features

    • Weighted, explainable scoring
    • Adjustable bot threshold
    • Live dashboard and charts
    • Local detection history

    Applications

    • Social platform moderation
    • Spam and fake-follower checks
    • Election misinformation research
    • Brand and influencer audits

    Advantages

    • Every result is explained
    • Fast, runs in the browser
    • No personal data leaves the device
    • Easy to tune and extend

    Limitations

    • Rules are hand-set, not trained on a large dataset
    • Human power users can look like bots
    • Bots evolve to look human
    • Uses numeric features only, not text
